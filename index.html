Why this book

Most assessment delays are not technical. They come from misunderstandings: what a term really means, what an assessor is actually asking for, and what “done” looks like. This guide is built around those mistakes, explains each one in plain language, and shows how to avoid it.

What you will learn

The terms people mix up

TAT, standard vs regulation, policy vs procedure vs control, and why “fixed” is not the same as “closed”.

Scoping that finishes

How to define and control scope so an assessment does not grow until it runs out of time and budget.

Evidence that holds up

How to request evidence, judge it, and spot what will be rejected.

Interviews and controls

Questions that get honest answers, and how to test whether a control is designed well and actually works.

Risk assessment and rating

A step-by-step process, clear rating scales, a risk matrix, and how to prioritise.

Reports people read

How to structure findings and write them so they are clear, specific, and hard to dispute.
